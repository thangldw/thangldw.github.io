I fix reliability problems in tools other people maintain. I keep information intact at its source, validate it at system boundaries, and add regression tests wherever a claim can be checked. I keep each change small enough to review and say where the evidence stops.

Each entry links to a pull request: either a direct merge recorded by GitHub, or an integration a maintainer confirmed with authorship preserved. Acknowledgments, direct commits, reviews and discussions are not listed. Credit belongs to the upstream maintainers and fellow contributors.
