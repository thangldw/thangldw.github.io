# JLPT prototype content

Original prototype lesson: negation scope, including 必ずしも〜ない, 全く〜ない and 全部が正しいわけではない. Original examples and two original questions; no existing licensed/protected corpus imported.

## References inspected on 12 October 2026

- Japan Foundation, BMA00015 必ずしも…否定: https://www.kyozai.jpf.go.jp/kyozai/material/BMA00015/ja/render.do — supports partial negation and teaching the expressions together.
- JLPT official test-section/item-type page: https://www.jlpt.jp/e/guideline/testsections.html — supports vocabulary/grammar/reading/listening capabilities. UI groups are study organization, not official section weights.
- NihongoNET authored grammar explanation わけではない: https://nihongokyoshi-net.com/2018/07/18/jlptn3-grammar-wakedewanai/?amp=1 — supports qualified denial and denial of an inferred conclusion. Its level label is not imported as an official classification.

Examples/questions were authored specifically for this prototype. They are not quoted official items, scored JLPT forms or release-ready reviewed corpus records. Each question is single-choice with explanation of every distractor; Vietnamese help retains the Japanese distinction. Reading passage is original. Recall writing is self-assessed. No audio and no listening questions are available. Native editor review and canonical governed importer remain prerequisites for any future production content adoption.
