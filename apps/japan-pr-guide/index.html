APPLICATION TYPE

Choose by where you are now

  • Outside Japan: the accepting organization or representative normally applies for a Certificate of Eligibility before the visa application.
  • In Japan under another status: apply for change of status before starting the HSP activity.
  • Already HSP: apply for extension before expiry; a material activity or organization change may require separate review.

CORE EVIDENCE

Prepare a verifiable chain

  • Correct 1号イ・ロ・ハ point table and enough prescribed evidence to establish at least 70 points.
  • Documents proving the proposed activity, organization, position, duties and expected annual remuneration.
  • Application form, photograph, passport and residence card as required by the selected procedure.
  • Attach Japanese translations to foreign-language documents; Japanese-issued certificates are generally expected to be recent.

EXCEPTIONNGOẠI LỆ

J-SKIP

Special Highly Skilled Professional — no point calculation

Academic / technical: master’s degree or higher plus ¥20,000,000 expected annual remuneration, or 10+ years’ relevant experience plus ¥20,000,000.

Business management: 5+ years’ management experience plus ¥40,000,000 expected annual remuneration.
